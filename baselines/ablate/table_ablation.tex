  \caption{\textbf{Ablations of \net on the Gold test set.}
  Each block changes one part of \net; its reference row is \net itself.
  (a) adds the 7,066 Silver clips to the training set; the last two rows score How on the test videos whose tool points the Silver generator never saw.
  (b) replaces the frozen V-JEPA~2.1 encoder and retrains the heads that read full-frame tokens; the zoom stage, the contact decoder, and the refiner are not retrained, so How is the stage-1 output after a 20-epoch schedule, the same for every encoder.
  The image encoders run on each frame, and the two frames of a V-JEPA tubelet are averaged.
  (c) scores the intermediate outputs of the Spatial Head and applies the refiner to the stage-1 points.
  (d) feeds the later heads the Semantic Head's predictions instead of the true tool and action; Which, What, and Pair give the accuracy of these conditions.
  (e) retrains only the contact decoder.
  Metrics as in \cref{tab:results}; Median: median tool-point error in pixels. Gray entries are placeholders.}
